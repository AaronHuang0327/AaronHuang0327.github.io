about

Surgeon, researcher, builder

My clinical work spans reconstructive microsurgery, hand surgery, aesthetic surgery and venous disease. In research, I focus on the decisions that shape surgical outcomes: how we monitor free flaps, how we predict who will do well, and how patients and surgeons choose between treatment options together.

During a research fellowship at NYU Langone Health, I worked on face transplantation and vascularized composite allotransplantation (VCA). Back in Taiwan, I combine that background with hands-on software work, from machine-learning models for flap monitoring to large language model tools for shared decision-making and workflow apps used in daily clinical practice.

research

Research themes

Clinical AI in reconstructive surgery

Machine-learning models for postoperative free-flap monitoring and outcome prediction, and evidence synthesis on how well AI tools perform in practice.

LLMs & shared decision-making

Patient-facing chatbots that support shared decision-making, and benchmarking how large language models handle real surgical decisions.

Patient-reported outcomes

Measuring what matters to patients after hand, digital and facial reconstruction, and building outcomes into how we compare techniques.

VCA & transplant immunology

Experimental VCA models, local immunosuppression strategies and clinical outcomes of face transplantation programs.

Hand & limb reconstruction

Vascularized toe joint transfer, nerve transfer, functional muscle transfer and orthoplastic limb salvage.

Wound care

Debridement, negative pressure wound therapy, chronic and arterial/venous wounds, and necrotizing soft tissue infection.
